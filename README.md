## What should you check before choosing an IPTV provider?

When comparing recommended IPTV providers, test more than the homepage.

Open the channels you actually care about. Try live sports during busy viewing hours. Check whether the EPG loads properly. Switch between a few devices if your household uses more than one. Then see how the service behaves when you are not sitting five feet from the router.

Also check the content rights for your location before paying for any independent IPTV service. IPTV is a delivery method; whether a particular provider is authorized to distribute specific channels or content is a separate question.
